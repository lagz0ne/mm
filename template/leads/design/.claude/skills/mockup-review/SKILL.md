# Mockup review (design lead)

Go through the mockup file and fix what fails before you ask for approval:
1. **States**: empty, loading, error, one item, many items (50+), very long title, many tags, are all shown.
2. **Grid**: one column system, consistent gutters; nothing aligns "by eye".
3. **Type**: at most 3 sizes plus 2 weights; numbers use tabular figures.
4. **Colour**: black and white plus one red accent, used only for emphasis or urgency; text contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
5. **Keyboard**: every action has a visible focus state; shortcuts are listed in the design doc.
6. **Phone**: at 390px wide nothing overflows and touch targets are ≥ 40px.
7. **Copy**: verbs on buttons; no lorem ipsum; error messages say what to do.

In the approval comment, list which checks you changed something for.
